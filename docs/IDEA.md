# The idea

SiteSync addresses the coordination gap in architecture, interior-design and construction projects. A change to one drawing or deliverable can affect several people, tasks and approvals, but these relationships are often scattered across messages, spreadsheets and individual knowledge. Teams may miss follow-ups or continue work using outdated assumptions.

The prototype connects stakeholders, task ownership, dependencies, change requests, review actions and approvals. When a change is recorded, a rule-based engine traces downstream work and explains each impact through its dependency path. The manager confirms the review plan, assigned stakeholders record what they checked, and the client approves or rejects the revised scope. Task prerequisites continue to apply after approval, and all important actions remain searchable in project history.

A residential-renovation scenario demonstrates a bathroom wall moving by 600 mm, affecting plumbing drawings, tile quantities, procurement and site work. The goal is clearer accountability and better visibility into what must happen next. The prototype provides explainable coordination intelligence without requiring runtime AI.
